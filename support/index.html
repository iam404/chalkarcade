Quick answers

Can I play without an internet connection?

Core game rules, levels and essential assets are included in the app. Supported solo, local and computer-opponent modes can work without a gameplay server. Sign-in, account sync, friend rooms, matchmaking and other online features need a connection.

What is the difference between a guest and an account?

When you continue as a guest with an internet connection, the app normally creates an online guest account, which can keep cloud data such as progress and any gaming-profile record. Without a connection it creates a local guest whose progress stays on that device. Guest access does not include all member features. An email account supports account recovery, cloud progress and supported friend features. Local guest history does not automatically merge into a later account unless the app explicitly offers that step. Chalk Arcade, including Guest Mode, is for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13.

I did not receive an email verification or recovery code.

Check the email address for typing errors, then check spam or junk folders. Use the newest code only and wait for the resend timer before requesting another. Never send the code to support or another player.

A private room or QR code is not working.

Confirm both players are online and using current compatible app versions. You can type the room code if camera access is denied. Private rooms expire, and a room can close if its host or a player leaves after play starts.

How do analytics and diagnostics work?

Settings contains two independent controls: Help improve Chalk Arcade for product analytics and Share crash diagnostics for crash reports. Both start on in a fresh mobile install, and you can turn either one off at any time. Your choices persist independently. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings → Privacy → Delete account, type DELETE, and choose Delete permanently. If you cannot access the app, use the email request on the Delete account page.

How do I remove guest data?

An online guest account has cloud data, including any Game Center or Google Play Games profile record, so delete it in the app first: Settings → Privacy → Delete account. Clearing storage or uninstalling only removes the guest session from the device. A local guest has no cloud account for us to delete. Clear Chalk Arcade's app storage in your device settings or uninstall the app. This permanently removes progress that exists only on that device.

How do I cancel or restore a purchase?

If paid products are offered in your version, purchases are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play account used to buy them. Use that storefront's subscription settings to cancel. Account deletion does not cancel a storefront subscription. Include the storefront transaction ID—not a full receipt or payment details—if you need entitlement help.

Can I change my player name or picture?

No. Chalk Arcade generates a stable player alias and draws a monogram locally. Other players do not see a user-editable display name or uploaded profile image. If someone misuses friend discovery or a private room, email support with “Player safety report” in the subject.

Privacy and legal requests

For access, correction, privacy, or data questions, email chalkarcade@prab.ch with “Privacy request” in the subject. For deletion, use the dedicated account-deletion instructions. We may ask you to verify account ownership, but never by sharing a password or one-time code.
